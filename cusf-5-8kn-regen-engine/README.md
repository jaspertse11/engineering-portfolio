# 5–8 kN regeneratively cooled engine

**Cambridge University Spaceflight (CUSF), Propulsion Team · 2025 – present · In design**

## Summary

A team of four is designing a 5–8 kN regeneratively cooled liquid rocket engine. The team has a preliminary design for the chamber, nozzle, cooling channels and impinging injector face, and the design is still being refined.

**My role:** I own the impinging injector face and the film-cooling orifices.

## What I am working on

- **Impinging injector face:** sizing and laying out the injector elements.
- **Film-cooling orifices:** sizing the orifices that supply the protective coolant film at the chamber wall.

## If I were to continue this project

- Analyse the coupled cooling system, including the regenerative channels and the film, against predicted wall temperatures.
- Hot-fire test a subscale version and compare measured against predicted performance.
